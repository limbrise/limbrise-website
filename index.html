Crutches haven't changed since Ancient Egypt.

Every year, 20 million North Americans injure their leg, and most end up on crutches that tie up both hands, cause secondary injuries, and slow them down. Nearly half give up on them entirely.

47%abandon their rehab protocol due to crutches
33%experience a secondary injury
2.4×slower than walking

Knee injuries have no hands-free option.

Hands-free crutches already exist, but only for foot and ankle injuries. Anyone with a knee injury is left on conventional crutches.

How LimbRise works

  1. Step 1

    Wear

    Straps securely to the lower leg and thigh. Adjustable across a target fit range.

  2. Step 2

    Load

    Redirects the load into your sitting bone, away from the injured limb.

  3. Step 3

    Move

    Move with both hands free. Stabilize your core like a yoga ball.

  4. Step 4

    Recover

    Maintain your daily life, without the setbacks that slow down recovery.

Designed with 5 surgeons, 6 physiotherapists, and 4 biomechanists.
